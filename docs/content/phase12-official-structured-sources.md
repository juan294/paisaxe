This is a local implementation and source-review record, not a publication receipt. ## Dates, attribution and terms

The three inspected captures supply no verified publisher update, publisher revision or product year. Those fields remain explicit nulls. Retrieval date is separate and cannot stand in for publisher freshness. The engineering refresh deadline is at most 90 days after acquisition and can be shortened following changed terms or contradictory identity evidence. A successful projection does not establish dataset completeness; truncated GIS responses are rejected and the retained responses remain `unmeasured` for complete-layer coverage.

The [tourism open-data terms](https://www.turismoasturiasprofesional.es/open-data) state a default CC BY 4.0 license unless otherwise indicated and expressly allow adaptation. They also retain conditional statutory reuse conditions, including unchanged information/metadata and last-update attribution. The separate site-wide reservations do not automatically override that dataset statement or license linked media. The [GIS FAQ](https://ideas.asturias.es/es/sitpa-ideas/preguntas-frecuentes) supplies a product-name/year attribution formula; the inspected metadata does not supply that product year.

The technical contract preserves source values unchanged, keeps normalization separate, labels the selected subset and independently authored writing, names the Administration and dataset, distinguishes unknown update from acquisition, and retains the source and license links in the citation. This minimizes alteration and attribution ambiguity; it is not a legal finding that every conditional requirement has been satisfied. The recorded source-specific disposition qualifies these exact unchanged fields for private factual authoring, based on the dataset/layer licenses, preserved values and supplied attribution. Missing unsupplied dates/year are explicitly unknown. This limited decision does not establish compliance with every final-publication condition; publication rights and promotion remain pending. No publisher correspondence is sent by this work.
