## Steps to name a hesitation

Use these labels so the five rows can be compared:

1. **Entrada:** the voucher page and opening the assistant.
2. **Petición:** saying what they want (people, day, budget, step-free need).
3. **Opciones:** reading the offer, including any option marked as not suitable or unconfirmed.
4. **Oferta:** the quote card and its "Aceptar oferta" button.
5. **Pago:** the payment card and PayPal's sandbox pages.
6. **Vuelta:** returning from PayPal to the booking page.
7. **Confirmación:** the booking page until "Reserva confirmada".

## Scoring the three questions

Correct (C), partly correct (P) or wrong (W), against the answers in `script.md`. Keep the
tester's own words in the row; the score goes in brackets after them.
