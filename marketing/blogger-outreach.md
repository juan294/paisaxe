# Travel Blogger Outreach

## Email Template

**Subject lines (pick one):**
- "Your [specific post title] inspired a project"
- "Quick question about your Asturias content"
- "Fellow Asturias enthusiast here"

---

**Email body:**

Hi [Name],

I came across your post on [specific article title]—[specific detail from the post that shows you actually read it, e.g., "your tip about the lesser-known viewpoint at Covadonga was spot-on"].

I've been working on a passion project called Paisaxe (paisaxe.com)—it's an AI-powered travel guide specifically for Asturias. You can chat with it about hiking routes, local restaurants, cultural sites, or even talk to it by voice like calling a local friend for tips.

I built it because I was frustrated with generic "top 10" travel content that doesn't answer specific questions. The AI is trained on local guides and actually cites its sources.

Would you be interested in checking it out? No pressure to write about it—I'd just genuinely value feedback from someone who knows the region. And if you do find it useful, feel free to share it however makes sense.

Either way, thanks for the content you put out. It's helped shape my own understanding of the region.

Best,
[Your name]

[Your website/social if relevant]

---

## Target Bloggers to Research

Look for bloggers who have written about:
- Northern Spain / Green Spain
- Camino de Santiago (especially Primitivo route)
- Picos de Europa hiking
- Spanish food/cider culture
- Off-the-beaten-path Spain

**Where to find them:**
- Google: "asturias travel blog", "picos de europa hiking blog"
- Pinterest: search Asturias, find blogs linked in pins
- Instagram: #asturias #picosdeeuropa → check if they have blogs

**Personalization checklist:**
- [ ] Read at least one full post from their site
- [ ] Reference something specific (not just "I loved your content")
- [ ] Check if they've covered Asturias before or just Spain generally
- [ ] Note their tone (casual vs. professional) and match it

- Track responses in a spreadsheet
- Don't follow up more than once
